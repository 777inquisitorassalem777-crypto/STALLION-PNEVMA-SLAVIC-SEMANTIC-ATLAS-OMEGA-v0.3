# Safety and epistemic integrity

- Simulation is the default.
- The semantic layer cannot directly actuate hardware.
- Real-world execution requires explicit human authorization.
- Harmful autonomous action is blocked.
- Integrity failure blocks execution.
- Scientific facts, hypotheses and symbolic interpretations are stored separately.
- "Quantum", "spiritual", "cosmological" and mythological concepts are not
  silently promoted to empirical facts.
- Historical/linguistic claims retain provenance and uncertainty.
- The system does not infer political, ethnic or national identity from
  semantic data.
